How it works

Three steps, then you forget about it.

No dashboard to check, no feed to scroll. The whole point is that it tells you, so you don't have to look.

01

Say what you're waiting for

In your own words. "Tell me when this flat gets RERA approval" is enough — no forms, no dropdowns.

02

We check, quietly

Every few hours we open the page, read it, and compare it to what it said last time. Nothing is sent unless something really changed.

03

You get one line

"The price fell from ₹61,000 to ₹54,300." Not a link. Not a feed. One sentence that tells you what to do.
