Refunds

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Wedge's paid plan is ₹100 for a year. It is a small amount, so we keep the refund rule simple rather than clever.

7 days, no questions

If you paid for the WhatsApp plan in the last 7 days and it has not done what you expected, write to ojasvkapoor@gmail.com from the number or email on your account and we will refund the full amount. You do not have to explain why.

After 7 days

We will still refund in these cases:

  • You were charged twice for the same year.
  • WhatsApp alerts never worked for you at all, and we could not fix it.
  • The service was unavailable for more than 30 days of your paid year.

In those cases we refund the full ₹100, or the unused part of the year, whichever is fairer to you.

When we do not refund

  • You changed your mind after 7 days and the service was working.
  • You hit the 100-alerts-a-month limit — that limit is stated before you pay.
  • Your account was closed for breaking the terms.

How it happens

Refunds go back to the same payment method you paid with, through Razorpay. We process them within 2 working days; your bank usually takes another 5 to 7 to show it. You will get a confirmation message when we have issued it.

Free plan

Nothing to refund — it costs nothing and always will.
