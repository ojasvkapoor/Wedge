Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

The short version

We keep a phone number, the pages you asked us to watch, and a log of what we sent you. We do not sell any of it. We do not read your messages. You can delete everything by asking.

What we do not collect

  • No location, no contacts, no photos.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no third-party analytics on the site.
  • We do not read messages you send to other people — only what you send to our bot.

Who else sees it

  • Meta processes the WhatsApp messages we send you, under its own terms.
  • Razorpay processes payments and holds your card details, not us.
  • Our hosting provider stores the database on our behalf.

We do not sell, rent or trade your data with anyone.

How long we keep it

While your account is active, and for 30 days after you delete it, so that a deletion can be undone if you ask. Payment records are kept for 8 years because Indian tax law requires it.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to see, correct, or delete your data, and you can withdraw consent at any time by stopping use of the service. Write to ojasvkapoor@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. If you are unhappy with our answer you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

Wedge is not for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

Data is stored on servers we control, access is limited, and payment signatures are verified cryptographically. No system is perfect; if there is a breach that affects you, we will tell you.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will message you before it takes effect.
